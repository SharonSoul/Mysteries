# Documentary Research

This skill directs topic discovery and deep historical research for **Mysteries & Unexplained History** documentaries, ensuring every film is grounded in reputable scholarship before scripting begins.

## Core Research Workflow

1. **Topic Identification & Scope Definition**:
   - Define the central historical question or anomaly (e.g., *What caused the Late Bronze Age Collapse?* or *Who built the underground city of Derinkuyu?*).
   - Establish geographic and chronological bounds.
2. **Primary Source Exploration**:
   - Locate primary historical chronicles, inscriptions, papyri, or contemporary accounts (e.g., Egyptian inscriptions at Medinet Habu).
   - Separate contemporary accounts from later historical interpretations.
3. **Archaeological & Forensic Evidence Gathering**:
   - Query peer-reviewed literature for excavation reports, stratigraphy, carbon dating, LiDAR scans, and material composition assays.
   - Prefer journals such as *Antiquity*, *Journal of Archaeological Science*, *American Journal of Archaeology*, and official institutional monographs.
4. **Scholarly Consensus vs. Alternative Hypotheses**:
   - Summarize the dominant academic consensus.
   - Document credible competing hypotheses.
   - Catalogue popular myths, Victorian misinterpretations, or fringe claims to be addressed and contextualized.
5. **Research Dossier Creation**:
   - Save findings into `research-library/<project-slug>/research_dossier.md`.

## Research Dossier Schema

```markdown
# Research Dossier: [Topic Title]
**Project Slug**: [slug]
**Date**: [YYYY-MM-DD]

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Mystery
[A concise overview of the anomaly, discovery, or historical event]

## 2. Chronological Timeline
- [Date/Era]: [Event / Construction / Disappearance]
- [Date/Era]: [Excavation / Rediscovery]

## 3. Physical & Archaeological Evidence
- **Artifact/Site 1**: [Description, excavation date, physical coordinates, current museum location]
- **Dating**: [Radiocarbon, dendrochronology, or epigraphic dating data]

## 4. Academic Theories & Debates
- **Theory A (Consensus)**: [Key scholars, supporting evidence, shortcomings]
- **Theory B (Alternative)**: [Key scholars, supporting evidence, criticisms]

## 5. Annotated Bibliography & Primary Citations
1. Author, A. (Year). *Title of Work*. Journal/Publisher. [URL/DOI]
```

# Documentary Quality Control

This skill defines the technical, legal, and editorial audit procedures that every documentary project must pass before it is considered production-complete.

## The Quality Control Checklist

### 1. Audio & Video Sync Verification
- Use `ffprobe` to inspect duration of the video stream (`v:0`) and audio stream (`a:0`).
- The duration difference between audio and video streams must not exceed **0.5 seconds**.
- No frozen frames at the end of the video while audio continues.

### 2. Audio Loudness Compliance (EBU R128)
- Run FFmpeg's `ebur128` filter to analyze integrated loudness:
  - **Target Integrated Loudness**: `-14.0 LUFS (±1.0 LUFS)`.
  - **Maximum True Peak**: `-1.0 dB TP`.
  - **Loudness Range (LRA)**: Between `5.0` and `10.0 LU`.
- Audio must not clip at `0.0 dBFS`.

### 3. Visual Asset Integrity
- Verify that every image is rendered at full target resolution (1920x1080 or 1080x1920) with no unintended pixelation or black letterbox borders unless stylized.
- Detect dropped or missing frames using FFmpeg's `blackdetect` filter.

### 4. Subtitle Timing & Legibility (Whisper Standard)
- Subtitles must be generated by local Whisper from the final approved narration audio, NOT directly from the script.
- Verify proper noun spellings, capitalization, and punctuation against historical research standards.
- Check that no subtitle cue has a display duration shorter than **1.0 second** or longer than **4.0 seconds**.
- Check that no two subtitle cues overlap in timecode.
- Check that characters per second (CPS) does not exceed **20 CPS**.

### 5. Licensing & Rights Audit
- Verify that 100% of visual and audio assets in the project storyboard have a corresponding entry in `licensing-records/<project-slug>_licensing_record.md`.
- Confirm that NO asset is marked with prohibited licenses (`CC BY-NC`, `CC BY-ND`, or unverified).
- Confirm that all required attributions are present in the video description metadata.

### 6. Human Approval Sign-off (Non-Negotiable)
- The autonomous system must pause and request user confirmation before rendering the final public export or packaging.
- **Never attempt to auto-upload to YouTube.**

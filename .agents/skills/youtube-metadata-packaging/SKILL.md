# YouTube Metadata & Release Packaging

This skill generates complete, publication-ready YouTube metadata packages for **Mysteries & Unexplained History** documentaries, optimizing for organic discoverability, viewer curiosity, and academic transparency.

## Human Approval Rule (Non-Negotiable)

**Never automatically upload, publish, or make public any video or metadata without explicit human review and approval.**
All metadata packages must be saved as Markdown files in `exports/<project-slug>/metadata.md` for human inspection.

## 1. Title Engineering

Create 3 distinct title angles for A/B testing. Titles must spark deep intellectual curiosity without resorting to false or misleading clickbait:

1. **The Anomaly / Discovery Angle**: Focuses on the physical object or sudden finding.
   - *Example*: The 2,000-Year-Old Greek Computer That Shouldn't Exist
2. **The Scientific Question Angle**: Focuses on the modern investigation.
   - *Example*: Why Modern Science Still Can't Explain the Antikythera Mechanism
3. **The Historical Narrative Angle**: Focuses on the forgotten past.
   - *Example*: The Lost Ancient Technology Recovered from the Aegean Sea

**Title Rules:**
- Keep primary title length between **45–60 characters** so it does not truncate on mobile screens.
- Avoid all-caps shouting or exaggerated hype words ("SHOCKING", "SECRET TRUTH REVEALED").
- Ensure the title reflects verified content within the video.

## 2. Thumbnail Concept & Art Direction

Thumbnails are visual hooks. A high-performing mystery documentary thumbnail requires:

- **Single Clear Focal Point**: One central, high-contrast element (e.g., a mysterious ancient gear, an isolated archaeological ruin under dramatic lighting, a glowing underwater excavation).
- **Text on Thumbnail**: Maximum **2 to 4 bold words** (e.g., "2,000 YEARS EARLY?", "FORGOTTEN GEARS", "IMPOSSIBLE RELIC").
- **Visual Palette**: Deep cinematic blues, teals, and obsidian shadows contrasted with warm amber, gold, or torchlight highlights.
- **Deliverable**: Provide a visual description prompt and text overlay blueprint for graphic designers or image generators.

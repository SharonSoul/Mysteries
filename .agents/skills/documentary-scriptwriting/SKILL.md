# Documentary Scriptwriting

This skill guides the authoring of production-ready documentary scripts for **Mysteries & Unexplained History**.

## Writing Principles

- **Visual-First Narrative**: Write for the eye as well as the ear. Describe what the audience sees before deciding what the narrator says.
- **Earned Curiosity**: Avoid sensationalist clickbait. Build tension through genuine historical enigmas, tangible physical evidence, and forensic contradictions.
- **Pacing**: Speak at a measured 130–140 words per minute. Leave space for music swells, ambient sound effects, and viewer reflection.
- **Pronunciation & Clues**: Provide phonetic guides for ancient names and archaeological sites in square brackets `[e.g. Göbekli Tepe (guh-BEK-lee TEH-peh)]`.

## Two-Column Audio/Visual (AV) Format

Every script must follow this production structure:

```markdown
# Script: [Documentary Title]
**Format**: [Shorts (9:16) / Longform (16:9)]
**Estimated Duration**: [XX minutes / seconds]
**Target Word Count**: [XXX words]

| Scene # & Timecode | Visual Direction (What We See) | Narration / Dialogue (What We Hear) | Audio Design (SFX / Music) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scene 01 (00:00 - 00:15)<br>**Cold Open Hook** | Macro shot of ancient eroded sandstone relief. Slow camera zoom toward a strange carving. Atmospheric dust motes drifting across the beam of sunlight. | *"Three hundred feet beneath the desert plateau, an expedition broke through a sealed chamber—and found something that defied every law of ancient engineering."* | Deep sub-bass pulse; distant desert wind howling softly. |
| Scene 02 (00:15 - 00:40)<br>**The Artifact** | Archival black-and-white excavation photograph from 1923 panning slowly from bottom to top. | *"It wasn't gold or jewelry. It was a granite block carved with sub-millimeter precision... in an era before hardened steel."* | Eerie sustained cello drone. Subtle stone-scraping SFX. |
```

## Formats & Pacing

### 1. YouTube Shorts (40–90s)
- **0–3s Hook**: Unsettling question or striking visual anomaly.
- **3–25s Context**: Why this discovery breaks historical consensus.
- **25–60s Evidence**: The key artifact, inscription, or modern CT scan.
- **60–80s Payoff / Open Question**: Unresolved conundrum and call to follow.

### 2. Long-Form Documentaries (10–15m)
- **Act I (Cold Open & Historical Context)**: The discovery and geographical setting.
- **Act II (The Anomaly & Physical Evidence)**: Deep dive into the artifacts and archaeological data.
- **Act III (Competing Hypotheses & Fact-Check)**: Scientific consensus vs. popular legends.
- **Act IV (Modern Science & Ongoing Mysteries)**: New insights from satellite LiDAR, DNA, or radiocarbon analysis.
- **Act V (Conclusion)**: What this teaches us about lost human capabilities.

# Production Storyboard: The Antikythera Mechanism
**Episode:** The 2,000-Year-Old Astronomical Computer  
**Format:** YouTube Short (9:16 Vertical, 1080x1920)  
**Total Scenes:** 5  
**Target Runtime:** ~65 seconds  

---

## Scene 1: The Sponge Diver Discovery (00:00 - 00:13)
* **Visual Asset:** `projects/antikythera-mechanism-short/assets/scene_01_antikythera_fragment_a.jpg` (1036x924, Fragment A preserved in Athens).
* **Framing:** 9:16 vertical canvas with blurred museum backdrop.
* **Camera Motion:** Slow push-in zoom (1.0x to 1.15x over scene duration) centered on the corroded green bronze drive wheel.
* **Transition:** Dip to deep navy blue (0.4s).
* **Narration:** *"In 1900, Greek sponge divers pulled a corroded lump of bronze from an ancient shipwreck. Hidden inside was an analog computer so advanced, modern science didn't believe the Greeks could build it until CT scans proved it in 2006."*
* **Sound Design:** Underwater acoustic drone, bubbles, followed by deep metallic impact on the reveal.

---

## Scene 2: The Impossible Internal Gears (00:13 - 00:28)
* **Visual Asset:** `projects/antikythera-mechanism-short/assets/scene_02_antikythera_teeth.jpg` (1036x924, Macro close-up of surviving triangular bronze gear teeth).
* **Framing:** 9:16 vertical canvas, top-lit dramatic framing.
* **Camera Motion:** Slow vertical pan upward following the triangular teeth line.
* **Transition:** Crossfade (0.5s).
* **Narration:** *"Found off the island of Antikythera, this machine was built over 2,100 years ago. Inside its calcified shell were more than thirty interlocking bronze gears with precision triangular teeth—a level of engineering history textbooks claimed didn't exist until 14th-century European clocks."*
* **Sound Design:** Rhythmic antique clockwork ticking layered with low harmonic drone.

---

## Scene 3: Calculating the Cosmos (00:28 - 00:43)
* **Visual Asset:** `projects/antikythera-mechanism-short/assets/scene_03_antikythera_dials_replica.jpg` (2301x1726, Detailed operational replica showing front and back calendar spirals).
* **Framing:** 9:16 vertical, centered on the Saros eclipse spiral.
* **Camera Motion:** Diagonal drift with subtle zoom-out (1.12x to 1.02x) revealing the multiple dial spirals.
* **Transition:** Crossfade (0.5s).
* **Narration:** *"It wasn't a timepiece. By turning a hand crank, it calculated solar eclipses decades in advance, tracked the Olympic Games, and used an epicyclic pin-and-slot gear train to replicate the elliptical speed of the moon."*
* **Sound Design:** High harmonic celestial shimmer, metallic gear engagement clicks.

---

## Scene 4: The 3,500 Character User Manual (00:43 - 00:54)
* **Visual Asset:** `projects/antikythera-mechanism-short/assets/scene_04_antikythera_inscriptions.jpg` (3405x3554, Raking light photograph of ancient Greek inscriptions on bronze door plates).
* **Framing:** 9:16 vertical canvas, macro detail on Greek letterforms.
* **Camera Motion:** Slow pan from top-left to bottom-right across the engraved columns.
* **Transition:** Soft crossfade (0.4s).
* **Narration:** *"Over 3,500 ancient Greek characters were engraved across its bronze plates, acting as a complete user manual. It wasn't built by aliens or myth. It was Hellenistic genius."*
* **Sound Design:** Music drops to near-silence for vocal gravity, single crisp bronze chime on "Hellenistic genius".

---

## Scene 5: The Lost Millennium & Outro (00:54 - 01:05)
* **Visual Asset:** `projects/antikythera-mechanism-short/assets/scene_05_antikythera_model.jpg` (4032x3024, Full reconstruction model of complete bronze mechanism in wooden casing).
* **Framing:** 9:16 vertical canvas, full apparatus visible.
* **Camera Motion:** Slow push-in zoom into the heart of the central differential gearing, followed by a slow fade to black.
* **Transition:** Fade to black (1.0s).
* **Narration:** *"Which leaves one haunting question: If humanity built a mechanical computer in 100 BC... where would our world be today if this technology had never been lost to the sea?"*
* **Sound Design:** Atmospheric strings crescendo, deep sub-bass drop, trailing into ocean wind resonance.
